Carbon targets WCAG 2.1 AA. Three rules do most of the work.

1 — Focus is a 2px ring in the focus colour, drawn inside the element
2 — Status never rests on colour alone: shape and text carry it too

3 — Contrast is measured in grade steps
Two colours from the same family need a gap of at least 50 grades to pass AA for body text (gray-100 on gray-10 = 90 steps, safe). Text at 24px+ or 19px bold can drop to a 40-step gap.
