Executive summary

Most organizations can build a model. Far fewer can defend one.

Across 1,200 surveyed organizations, 78% had at least one foundation model in production. Only 31% could produce a complete lineage record for that model when asked — covering training data provenance, evaluation results, approval chain and post-deployment drift.

That gap is not primarily technical. Organizations that closed it did not buy a different model; they changed the sequence in which they built things, putting evidence collection before scale rather than after it.

Key finding. Organizations that instrumented governance before their second production model reached audit readiness 3.4× faster than those that retrofitted it — and spent 22% less in total.
